1. Files (https://github.com/files-community/Files)
---------------------------------------------------
Used: the sidebar section icons (res/sidebar), the UI translations taken from Files' Strings/*/Resources.resw
(res/lang.txt, generated by tools/gen-lang.ps1), and Files' behavior, layout metrics and texts as the design
reference; some of Files' logic (e.g. sorting, grouping and date labels) was ported to C++ and Rust. FastFiles is a
native reimplementation and contains none of Files' C# / XAML source files.
